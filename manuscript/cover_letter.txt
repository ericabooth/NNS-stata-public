To the Editors
The Stata Journal

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed article, "nnsreg: Reportable segment slopes for
nonlinear relationships in Stata," for publication in the Stata Journal.

The article introduces nnsreg, a new command that fits a univariate Nonlinear
Nonparametric Statistics (NNS) regression and posts the result as ordinary
Stata estimation results. Its contribution is one of software and workflow
rather than a new estimator: the command turns a fitted nonlinear curve into a
small set of reportable, lincom-testable segment slopes with data-derived knot
locations, so that a shape a smoother can only draw becomes a few reproducible
statements about where a relationship is flat, steep, or changing. Three
simulated designs with known curves, a 100-replication Monte Carlo study, and
a cross-validated real-data illustration place the command honestly: the
npregress smoothers recover smooth curves at least as accurately on average,
while nnsreg adds a reportable segment summary and is steadier on clustered
support. The article positions nnsreg among Stata's existing nonlinear-fit and
change-point tools and is explicit about what it does and does not provide.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not
been published previously. I am the sole author.

The submission is a flat, self-contained packet. nnsreg.tex compiles in place,
and the included do-files, datasets, ado-files, and help file reproduce every
example, figure, and table; the readme.txt file describes the contents and the
reproduction steps. Figures are supplied as grayscale PDFs.

I would be glad to answer any questions. Thank you for your consideration.
