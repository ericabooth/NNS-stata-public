# Receipts: nnsreg Stata Journal manuscript

Thesis (one sentence): nnsreg brings the established NNS partition-based regression of Viole and Nawrocki to Stata as an inspectable line-segment fit with full estimation-results integration; simulations and a 100-rep Monte Carlo show kernel regression wins smooth designs while the NNS OLS spline wins clustered designs on both accuracy and stability.
